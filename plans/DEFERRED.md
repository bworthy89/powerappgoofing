# Deferred ideas

Considered and consciously not built, with the reasoning at the time — so a later decision
starts from what was already thought through rather than from scratch.

## Technicians suggest, an admin confirms

**Considered** 2026-08-23, while making data maintenance easier.
**Status** deferred; admin-only writing stays for now.

A technician at a machine submits what they found — "this is on 3.6.0" — from the machine's
own screen. It queues as a pending suggestion; an admin accepts or rejects it. Maintenance
becomes a byproduct of the visit instead of a separate chore, while one person stays
accountable for what is recorded.

**Why it is attractive** The most frequent real update is a version after a service visit,
and the person who knows it is standing at the machine. Today that knowledge has to survive
the trip back to someone with admin access.

**What it costs** A pending state on the installation, a review queue for admins to watch,
and a rule for when two technicians suggest different values. None of it is hard; all of it
is new surface, and none of it is needed to make the current admin screens easier.

**Revisit when** Admins become the bottleneck, or version data goes stale between visits.
